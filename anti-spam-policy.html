Anti-Spam Policy

Talex Media LLC does not send unsolicited commercial email advertising gotalexmedia.com, and does not permit anyone else to do so on our behalf.

Who we email

We write to named individuals at business addresses about work we believe is relevant to them. We do not send bulk mail, and we do not send to anyone who has asked us not to.

Lists

We do not buy, rent, scrape, harvest, or exchange email lists. We do not send to addresses obtained from any third-party list vendor.

Third parties and affiliates

We have no affiliate program. No agency, contractor, reseller, or other third party is authorized to promote this domain by email. Anyone who does so is acting without our permission and against this policy, and we will act to stop it as soon as we are made aware.

Opting out

Every message we send identifies Talex Media, comes from a named person at a working reply address, and can be stopped by replying. We honor opt-out requests immediately and permanently. We keep a suppression list and do not re-add anyone to it.

Reporting a problem

If you received email from this domain that you did not ask for, please send it to us with full headers at evan.talex@gotalexmedia.com. We will investigate it, remove the address, and tell you what we found.
